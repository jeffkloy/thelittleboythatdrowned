# Trying To Convince Myself That I'm Not A Bad Person

I was going to write a poem about making love:
'I'm going to make love to you every day
And I don't mean sex
Of course, that'd be wonderful too, but
I mean I'm going to embody love into all the things that I do for you'

But instead of that poem, I've been trying to convince myself that I'm not a bad person
Because at 3:30 that October morning, I abandoned you
Broke and homeless in a country we both had no ties to
I lost my most important family member for you
I lost most of my own self-respect for you
And so I did it because I had already given everything to you
And so I did it because I had nothing left to give
All I got from you were broken promises
All I got from you was disrespect
But most of all, all I got from you was a false sense of being cherished

People have told me that I'm too much
People have told me that I'm draining
I know how it feels to be the one they walk away from
So I know what I did to you
My deep-seated fear of abandonment
I know exactly what I did to you

And I've always felt that I was a little boy that drowns
Because when he was drowning, a hand grabbed him, and in a second, it let him go
And a voice, it said to him, 'I'm not the one to help you'
I think I've despised that hand my entire life
And at 3:30 that October morning, I finally became the hand
But maybe the hand was never bad
Maybe the hand was going under too
Maybe it let go because it wanted so badly to live

All I ever wanted was to be chosen, just once
Not to be loved out of convenience
Not to be loved out of your self-preservation
So at 3:30 that October morning, I finally chose myself
