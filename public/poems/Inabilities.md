# "Inabilities"

I open the door in front of me
Slowly pace my way into the room
A woman I barely know is at a table
As I bee-line my way towards her lovely presence
I practiced this walk the whole way here
I practice everything

To think of all the things I can and cannot do
I can't hold up a household
I can't hold down a full-time job
I can't even keep a healthy conversation going
At least not without a script I wrote the night before:
Eye contact in three-second doses
Thirty-seven years of studying people
And I still need something in my blood to pass for one

It's not long before my mind goes for the gutter
A hundred things that I'll hide from her
My addictions:
What I snorted on my way over so my brain would sit still
My desires:
To be loved by someone who's met the actual me
My emergencies:
The mornings I woke up surprised to still be alive

What if she hates me?
Or worse, what if she falls for me?
What if it's the performance she falls for?
The man I built on the sidewalk
While the real me waits outside
High, stimming, unbearable
Out there still practicing how to say hello
